Graphical abstract

The abstract shows Tool4Boxology as three connected layers: a human-readable Boxology design layer, a machine-readable knowledge representation layer, and an exploration layer for analysis and querying.

1. Design

Make the architecture explicit.

The diagram captures symbolic and neural components in a common Boxology notation, so humans can read and discuss the system.

2. Represent

Turn validated designs into graph data.

The design becomes a knowledge graph that preserves patterns, components, processes, inputs, and outputs.

3. Explore

Query, analyze, and reuse.

SPARQL and the KG viewer make the architecture machine-readable and easier to compare across systems.
