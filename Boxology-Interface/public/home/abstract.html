Graphical abstract

A layered framework for constructing and analyzing neuro-symbolic systems that integrate symbolic and neural components. The design layer ensures syntactic correctness, the knowledge representation layer transforms validated designs into knowledge graphs, and the exploration layer enables querying and analysis.
